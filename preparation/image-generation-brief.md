## Notes for the Agent

- **Style:** All images should feel editorial and cinematic, NOT stock photography
- **People:** Avoid tourists entirely. Monks/priests are acceptable if candid and non-posed
- **Colour:** Images will be used on a dark slate (`#0F172A`) background — darker, moodier tones work best
- **Format:** JPEG, quality 85–90%, progressive loading preferred
- **File naming:** snake_case as listed above, place in `assets/images/`
