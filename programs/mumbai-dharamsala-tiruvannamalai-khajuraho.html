Mountain, Cave, Temple & Cosmos

Grand Traverse Route

The Program Itinerary

An intellectually rigorous traverse through the mountain ranges, coastal caves, and core sanctuaries of the Indian subcontinent.

DAY 01
DAY 01

Mumbai: Coastal Portals & Monastic Buddhism

Arrival in coastal Mumbai. Welcome transfers to the historic Palace Hotel facing the Arabian Sea. In the afternoon, a private excursion to the Kanheri Caves in the national park, where we analyze the early Buddhist scriptures and monastic stone inscriptions dating to the 1st Century BCE.

Lodging: The Taj Mahal Palace (Tower Wing) Meals: Welcome Dinner
DAY 02
DAY 02

Mumbai to Dharamshala: Himalayan Ascent

Morning flight into the Himalayan foothills of Kangra Valley, followed by a scenic drive to Dharamshala. We check in at our luxury mountain resort surrounded by pine forests. Evening fire-side briefing on Himalayan Buddhist lineages.

Lodging: Hyatt Regency Dharamshala Resort Meals: Breakfast & Dinner
DAY 03
DAY 03

Dharamshala: Tibetan Semantics & Philosophy

We visit the Norbulingka Institute, dedicated to preserving Tibetan cultural arts. Svagato leads a seminar on the translation paths from Sanskrit to classical Tibetan, explaining how abstract philosophical concepts were structurally coded into the language.

Lodging: Hyatt Regency Dharamshala Resort Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
DAY 04
DAY 04

Dharamshala: Forest Silence & Monastic Debates

A morning silent walk through oak forests. In the afternoon, we attend the active dialectic debating sessions of the monks at the Tsuglagkhang Temple complex, witnessing the physical and logical structure of Tibetan philosophical analysis.

Lodging: Hyatt Regency Dharamshala Resort Meals: Breakfast & Dinner
DAY 05
DAY 05

Dharamshala to Tiruvannamalai: The Southern Transition

We descend Kangra Valley and board our flight south. Arriving in Chennai, a private luxury coach drives us inland to the ancient town of Tiruvannamalai, situated at the base of the sacred red mountain Arunachala.

Lodging: Sparsa Eco-Luxury Resort Meals: Breakfast & Dinner
DAY 06
DAY 06

Tiruvannamalai: Arunachala Circumambulation

At dawn, we perform the 14-kilometer Giri Pradakshina circumambulation around Arunachala Hill. Svagato explains the concept of "Sacred Geography" and details the history of this mountain as a traditional energetic pillar of fire.

Lodging: Sparsa Eco-Luxury Resort Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
DAY 07
DAY 07

Tiruvannamalai: The Silence of Advaita

We spend the day at the Ramana Maharshi Ashram, participating in silent mediation. In the afternoon, we hike to the Virupaksha Cave, where Ramana Maharshi spent years in complete silence, to study the Advaita Vedanta philosophy of self-inquiry ("Who am I?").

Lodging: Sparsa Eco-Luxury Resort Meals: Breakfast & Dinner
DAY 08
DAY 08

Tiruvannamalai: Fire Temple Iconography

An in-depth visit to the Arunachaleswarar Temple, one of the largest temple complexes in India. We examine the architecture representing the fire element (Agni), and trace the Sanskrit chants and classical sound designs within the massive stone halls.

Lodging: Sparsa Eco-Luxury Resort Meals: Breakfast & Dinner
DAY 09
DAY 09

Tiruvannamalai to Khajuraho: Central Transition

Drive to Chennai Airport, followed by a domestic flight to Khajuraho (via Delhi). Arrive in the quiet town of Khajuraho in Madhya Pradesh, checking in to our resort adjacent to the temples.

Lodging: The Lalit Temple View Khajuraho Meals: Breakfast & Dinner
DAY 10
DAY 10

Khajuraho: The Tantric Mandalas

We begin our exploration of the Western Group of Temples (Lakshmana and Kandariya Mahadeva). Svagato decodes the architectural plan of these temples, which are built as 3D mandalas representing the union of cosmic and physical energies.

Lodging: The Lalit Temple View Khajuraho Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
DAY 11
DAY 11

Khajuraho: Deciphering Erotic Artistry

A detailed study of the famous erotic sculptures of Khajuraho. Rather than simple carvings, Svagato translates the Sanskrit verses associated with Tantra, showing how these figures symbolize the integration of intellect, emotion, and spiritual transcendence.

Lodging: The Lalit Temple View Khajuraho Meals: Breakfast & Dinner
DAY 12
DAY 12

Khajuraho: Rural Sanctuaries & Sound Meditations

A quiet day visiting the less-frequented Eastern (Jain) and Southern temple groups. We hold an afternoon outdoor sound meditation session by the Khodar river, preparing our minds for the final travel integration.

Lodging: The Lalit Temple View Khajuraho Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
DAY 13
DAY 13

Khajuraho to Mumbai: Integration Dinner

We fly from Khajuraho back to Mumbai. Check-in once more at the sea-facing Taj Mahal Palace. In the evening, Svagato hosts a private farewell banquet, summarizing the dialectics between Northern mountain philosophy and Southern temple traditions.

Lodging: The Taj Mahal Palace (Tower Wing) Meals: Breakfast & Farewell Feast
DAY 14
DAY 14

Mumbai: Departure

Late morning coffee and final reflections in the hotel. Private transfers are organized to Mumbai International Airport for your onward flights home.

Complete Expedition Meals: Breakfast
